Game GDD (Game Design Document)

Game Title: Lumi
Genre: 2D Platformer / Puzzle (Includes Auto-Runner Elements)

Elevator Pitch:
Lumi is an endless auto-runner where you must outsmart pursuing enemies using perfectly timed abilities and quick platform drawing. The player doesn't control movement — instead, they react to danger by dropping decoys, jumping over obstacles, activating shields, and drawing platforms with the mouse to fill gaps. Every second counts; one wrong move means capture or a fatal fall.

Core Gameplay Loop:
Continuously Advance → Spot Danger → Use the Right Ability (Key) on Time → Draw a Path (Platform) for Gaps → Reach the Goal.

Controls and Mechanics:
Lumi moves forward automatically. The player intervenes based on the situation as follows:

Platform Drawing (Keyboard + Mouse):
- Z + Left Mouse Click: By holding the Z key and clicking the left mouse button, a temporary platform is placed at the targeted location. This is the only mechanic in the game that utilizes the mouse.

Lumi's Instant Abilities (Keyboard Only):
- X – Drop Decoy: Lumi drops a decoy at their current position and keeps running. Pursuing enemies will stop chasing Lumi and get distracted by the decoy.
- C – Jump: Lumi throws a jump pad right in front of them and immediately bounces off it to vault over obstacles or enemies.
- V – Shield: Lumi creates an instant shield around themselves to block one instance of incoming damage.

Win-Loss Conditions:
- Win: Lumi successfully runs non-stop and reaches the safe goal at the end of the level.
- Loss: Lumi falls into a gap, collides with an obstacle, or gets caught by an enemy.

Art Style:
Minimalist 2D / Vector Design (Dark space theme and a flat, muted color palette).
