Forest Friends Freebie Pack - Pixel Art by Pixel Hollow Studios
(Featuring Lark and their owl companion)

Thank you for checking out this freebie from Pixel Hollow Studios!

This cozy little teaser was made to share a taste of the Forest Friends Starter Pack—crafted with warmth, whimsy, and solo devs in mind. Whether you're prototyping or just starting your journey, we hope these sprites help bring a bit of magic to your world.

---

Included in This Pack:

Lark — A red-hooded adventurer ready to explore your cozy forest

Owl Companion — Loyal and watchful, fluttering by their side
Note: This free pack includes only the base versions (no color variants).


All files are provided as transparent PNGs and organized for easy use.

---

Specs:

File Type: PNG

Canvas Size: 48×48 pixels

Animation: Static sprites only (no animations included)

Variants: None included in this freebie (variants are available in the full pack)
